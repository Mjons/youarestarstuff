WHERE YOU CAME FROM, ONE ATOM AT A TIME.

The universe is the longest origin story there is, and you are the latest chapter. We turn real science into short, beautiful episodes anyone can watch, so the biggest story ever told feels like your own.

Every frame is drawn from hexagons, the shape nature keeps choosing: honeycombs, snowflakes, carbon rings.
